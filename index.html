Garmin WHOOP Performance

Privacy Policy

This policy describes the processing of data by Garmin WHOOP Performance, a personal, locally run training and recovery dashboard (the “App”). The App is an independent project, not an official WHOOP or Garmin product.

Effective date: October 30, 2026

1. Operator and purpose

The App is operated privately for personal training analysis and planning. It is currently intended for use by its operator only, not offered as a public service.

2. Data accessed

With your permission, the App may retrieve WHOOP data permitted by the scopes you approve, including recovery metrics, heart rate variability (HRV), resting heart rate, sleep records, physiological cycle metrics, strain, and workout data. It may combine those records with Garmin training data to show trends and support training planning. The App requests only the scopes needed for enabled features.

3. Authentication and storage

WHOOP account access uses WHOOP's OAuth authorization process. WHOOP authenticates users on its own service. Access credentials and downloaded fitness data are intended to be stored locally on the operator's device, not in this public privacy-policy repository. The App does not require users to provide WHOOP passwords directly to the App.

4. Sharing and third parties

The operator does not intentionally sell WHOOP data, use it for advertising, or transmit it to analytics providers. Requests to WHOOP and Garmin are necessary to retrieve their respective data. Those services process information under their own terms and privacy notices. This privacy-policy webpage is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical visit information under GitHub's own policies.

5. Retention, deletion, and access

Downloaded records and tokens remain on the local device until the operator deletes them. The operator can delete locally stored records and credentials and revoke authorization through WHOOP's account or integration controls. Deleting local data does not delete records retained by WHOOP or Garmin.

6. Security

The App is designed for local development. API credentials should be kept in environment files or local secure storage and excluded from public source-code repositories. No security guarantee is made; access to the device and its files should be protected.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated if the App's functionality, data use, or hosting changes. The effective date above will be revised when appropriate.
